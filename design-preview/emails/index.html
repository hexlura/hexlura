EMAIL REDESIGN

Your 30 transactional emails still use the old look: a dark header, a grey page, heavy Arial headings and emoji icons. These mockups bring them into the new warm style. Nine of them are drawn in full, one for each pattern of message you send. The other 21 follow the same patterns (table at the bottom).

All names, events and amounts are fictional sample data. Wording is kept exactly as it is today, except where a note says otherwise. Use the Desktop / Mobile toggle inside each mockup.

WHAT CHANGES

Today

  • · Near-black header bar (#0A0A0F) on a cold grey page (#F5F5F7)
  • · Arial Black headings; boxes with sharp corners
  • · Emoji as icons (render differently in every email app)
  • · Flat red button with no breathing room
  • · Footer: company name, number, support email — no address

Redesign

  • · Warm cream page, white rounded card, red→orange strip along the top
  • · Bebas Neue headings with a safe fallback; DM Sans body
  • · Tinted icon circles (green / red / amber) instead of emoji
  • · Soft cream info cards, rounded buttons with a gentle glow
  • · One shared footer with the company number and registered office address

RULES FOR THE BUILD

These mockups are a visual guide, not email-ready code. Email apps are far fussier than browsers, so the real build must follow these rules:

  • One shared layout. Change emails/components/BaseEmail.tsx once (header, footer, colours) and all 30 templates update.
  • Tables and inline styles (React Email already does this). No flexbox or CSS variables in the final emails.
  • Fonts. Gmail and Outlook don't load web fonts. Headings must look fine in the fallback (Impact / Arial Narrow), so design with that in mind. Body text is Arial.
  • Icons are hosted PNGs (about 2× size), not emoji or SVG. Email apps block SVG.
  • The top gradient strip falls back to solid red in Outlook, which ignores gradients. The rounded corners will square off in desktop Outlook. Both are acceptable.
  • Buttons use the "bulletproof button" pattern, so they stay clickable when images are off.
  • Test before sending in Gmail, Apple Mail and Outlook, light and dark mode, with a real send to your own address.
  • The fee rule still applies. Organiser emails show ticket revenue only. Buyer and admin emails may show fees.

THINGS I FOUND WHILE READING THE EMAILS

  1. Ticket lines show the wrong price. The buyer confirmation and the organiser alert print "2 × £41.98" and "2 × £40.00", but those figures are the line totals, not the per-ticket price. This is a real bug in today's emails, and the mockups show it correctly.
  2. The footer has no registered office address. UK companies normally have to show their registered name, company number and registered office in business emails. The mockups add it. Please confirm with your accountant or lawyer.
  3. Fixed: "do not reply" vs "just reply". Six emails told people to reply while the footer said not to. They now say "contact support@hexlura.com".
  4. Fixed: "keep 100% of face value". Removed from the user and organiser welcome emails, because it stops being true after the Stripe change. The public site and Terms still say it and need updating when that change happens.
  5. The payout email always says "bank transfer". Stripe Connect organisers are paid automatically and don't get this email.
  6. Marketing emails need an unsubscribe link. Announcement, event-promo-campaign and new-event-followers are marketing. I have not checked whether they include one.
  7. Not yet in git: emails/event-correction.tsx exists only on your PC.

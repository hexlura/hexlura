ABOUT HEXLURA

OUR STORY

Hexlura started with a simple frustration: buying and selling event tickets shouldn't be this complicated. We set out to build a platform that's fast for buyers and fair for organisers — no bloated fees, no clunky checkout, no unnecessary friction.

OUR MISSION

To make live events more accessible — helping people discover experiences worth showing up for, and helping the organisers behind them grow sustainably.

FOR BUSINESS

Whether you're running a warehouse rave, a comedy night, or a community market, Hexlura gives you the tools to sell tickets, manage attendees, and get paid — all in one place, with payouts settled directly to your own Stripe account.

FOR BUYERS

Discover events happening near you, book in seconds, and keep your tickets safe with QR codes delivered straight to your inbox — no app download required.

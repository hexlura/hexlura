HOW IT WORKS

FOR TICKET BUYERS

1

Find an event

Browse by city, category, or search for something specific.

2

Select tickets

Choose your ticket type and quantity from the event page.

3

Pay securely

Checkout with card via Stripe — no account required.

4

Get your PDF ticket

Your ticket with QR code is emailed instantly and available to download anytime.

5

Show up and scan in

Present your QR code at the door for instant check-in.

FOR EVENT ORGANISERS

1

Create an account

Sign up and apply as an organiser — free to start.

2

Connect Stripe

Link your Stripe account so you can receive payouts directly.

3

Create your event

Add ticket tiers, banner images, refund policy, and publish.

4

Share and sell

Promote your event page directly or via promoters and email campaigns.

5

Manage attendees and check-in

Track sales in real time and scan tickets at the door.

6

Get paid

Funds settle directly to your connected Stripe account after each sale.

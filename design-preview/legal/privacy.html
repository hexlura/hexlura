PRIVACY POLICY

This content is published and versioned by Hexlura admins. This mockup shows the general structure only — real copy is managed via the admin legal editor.

WHO WE ARE

Hexlura Ltd (Company No. 17102803) is the data controller for personal information collected through this platform. We are in the process of registering with the ICO (registration pending).

WHAT WE COLLECT

Account details (name, email, phone), booking history, payment metadata (via Stripe — we never store full card numbers), device and usage data, and any information you provide via support tickets or reviews.

HOW WE USE YOUR DATA

To process bookings, send booking confirmations and event reminders, provide customer support, and — where you've opted in — send marketing communications.

SHARING YOUR DATA

We share booking details with the relevant event organiser so they can manage attendees and check-in. We use Stripe for payment processing and Resend for transactional email delivery.

YOUR RIGHTS

You can request a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, or withdraw marketing consent at any time from your Account Settings page.

DATA RETENTION

We retain booking records as required for accounting and legal purposes, and delete account data upon request unless retention is legally required.

CONTACT

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to support@hexlura.com.

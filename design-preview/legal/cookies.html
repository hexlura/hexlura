COOKIE POLICY

This content is published and versioned by Hexlura admins. This mockup shows the general structure only — real copy is managed via the admin legal editor.

We use cookies to run the site, remember your preferences, and understand how Hexlura is used. Below is a breakdown of the cookies we use.

Session Cookie

ESSENTIAL

Keeps you logged in as you browse and book events.

CSRF Token

ESSENTIAL

Protects forms and checkout from cross-site request forgery.

Stripe Cookies

ESSENTIAL

Set by Stripe during checkout for fraud prevention. Third-party — see Stripe's own cookie policy.

Preferences Cookie

FUNCTIONAL

Remembers display preferences, such as your last-used city filter.

Checkout State

FUNCTIONAL

Preserves your ticket selection if you navigate away mid-checkout.

Google Analytics

ANALYTICS

Helps us understand aggregate site usage. Third-party — can be disabled via browser settings.

Meta Pixel

ANALYTICS

Used by some organisers to measure the performance of their event promotions. Third-party.

Performance Monitoring

ANALYTICS

Helps us detect and fix errors on the platform.

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time. Disabling essential cookies may prevent checkout from working correctly.

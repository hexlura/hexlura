Accent red stays the primary brand colour. Orange pairs with it for gradients (accent → orange); amber, yellow and green complete the data set. Tinted chips use the colour at 10% opacity (e.g. bg-accent/10) with the colour — or its Text variant — as the foreground. The canvas carries two soft radial washes (orange top-right, red top-left).
